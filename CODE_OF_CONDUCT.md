# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive,
assume good intent, welcome different experience levels, and focus criticism on
ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hi@valensto.com`. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when needed
to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
